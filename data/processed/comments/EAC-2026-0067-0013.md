# EAC-2026-0067-0013 — Stephen Aleshire

**Date:** 2026-05-20
**Docket:** EAC-2026-0067

---

Election audits need to be performed by a disinterested third party that is arms length from election poll workers, election data aggregators working for the jurisdiction of concern, secretary of states, and candidates and their staff. Audits should be statistically meaningful, not so-called risk limiting audits, which have been repeatedly shown to be statically irrelevant and in fact misleading. The most important is that merely counting paper ballots by the same poll workers or candidates is inappropriate and invalid.
