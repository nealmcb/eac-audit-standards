# EAC-2026-0067-0046 — John Cardarelli

**Date:** 2026-05-20
**Docket:** EAC-2026-0067
**Source:** [Regulations.gov](https://www.regulations.gov/comment/EAC-2026-0067-0046)

For auditing to be accurate and sound, a hand count must be used to compare with a machine count. This hand count could be done by bi-partisan, reputable local people who might be chosen by a random procedure such as a lottery.
