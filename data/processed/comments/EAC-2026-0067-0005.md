# EAC-2026-0067-0005 — Anonymous (Civera)

**Date:** 2026-05-20
**Docket:** EAC-2026-0067

---

Civera (https://civera.com) currently offers a product called ElectionShield, which uses statistics to detect anomalies or irregularities in election results. We would like to see a statistical/forensics analysis of election results become a standard security practice for all elections in the US. Such detected anomalies can then be investigated in the field to uncover any particular root cause. Such a technique has been used to detect operational errors, and worse: "ballot box stuffing" in Philadelphia resulting in the criminal conviction of former US Congressman Ozzie Myers: https://www.justice.gov/usao-edpa/pr/former-us-congressman-and-philadelphia-political-operative-sentenced-30-months-prison
