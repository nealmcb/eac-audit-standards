# EAC-2026-0067-0057 — Anonymous (MTX Group)

**Date:** 2026-05-20
**Docket:** EAC-2026-0067
**Source:** [Regulations.gov](https://www.regulations.gov/comment/EAC-2026-0067-0057)

MTX Group applauds the Election Assistance Commission for publishing these Audit Standards. We have but one comment regarding this new document: Page 31, under the section Public Observation there is a comment that "attendance is often low even when observation is offered". While this statement may be accurate it does not convey the positives of public observation and maintain the overall tenor of the document. We believe this second half of the sentence should be deleted. Once again we feel this is a much-needed standard and are glad to see it published. Thank you for the opportunity to make public comments.
