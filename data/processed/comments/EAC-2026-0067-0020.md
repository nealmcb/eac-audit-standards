# EAC-2026-0067-0020 — Gregory Lawson

**Date:** 2026-05-20
**Docket:** EAC-2026-0067

---

Audits should be performed by a party that is independent from the County Auditor's office. At the least, it should be done by the state Auditor. Ideally, it should be done by two separate parties from out of state.
