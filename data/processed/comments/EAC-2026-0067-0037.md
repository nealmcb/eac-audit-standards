# EAC-2026-0067-0037 — Gary Chick

**Date:** 2026-05-20
**Docket:** EAC-2026-0067
**Source:** [Regulations.gov](https://www.regulations.gov/comment/EAC-2026-0067-0037)

---

In reading through your document, it appears that you're advocating for Elections Departments to be the ones to audit their elections. Although you label this as a voluntary audit, I think the goal should be that audits be required by law. Even for voluntary audits, the audits should be done by third parties. Third party audits are required in several industries, particularly financial, publicly traded companies and entities receiving certain levels of government grant money. In all these instances, the companies are audited by a third party. In fact, it would seem almost ridiculous to allow them to audit themselves. You mention concerns over sensitive voter data. Auditors for various industries also work with data just as sensitive, if not more sensitive. I do think this is a good idea, but I believe you should stress the confidence that third party audits will provide.
