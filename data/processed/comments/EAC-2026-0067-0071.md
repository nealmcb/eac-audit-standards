Radical Legibility To restore the Republic, we must end the era of Administrative Priestcraft and return to a system where the burden of proof is met through Radical Legibility. This is not a step backward into “inefficiency,” but a step forward into Security and Sovereignty. 1. Tearing the Veil The only way to verify the validity of an election is to remove the digital intermediary. We must replace the proprietary “black box” with a process that is observable by any Citizen without specialized equipment or secret code. A manual, precinct-level hand count is the only method that satisfies the requirement of public proof. 2. Restoring the Master-Servant Relationship When the count is performed in the light of day by the Neighbors of the Precinct, the government is once again the servant, demonstrating the count to the People. The burden of proof is satisfied in real-time. There is no “crime scene” to be denied entry to, because the Citizen is a witness to the entire process. 3. Verification over Trust A Constitutional Republic does not ask for “faith” in a machine; it demands verification of the truth. By moving from a hidden “tabulation industry” to a public “tabulation ceremony,” we restore the validity of the count and, by extension, the legitimacy of the government.
