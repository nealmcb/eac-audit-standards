Though the committee has done excellent work on this, I believe your document is too long. It gets into the weeds when you only need a field view. Suggest that each state and county create an Audit Committee consisting of current election officials, former election officials, and several members-at-large that will determine the details (explanations, details, procedures, etc.) of election audits. The Committee will also hire an independent auditor from out of state to conduct each audit. A different auditor will be hired for each election cycle. Each member of the Audit Committee shall be replaced every two years (use a rotation system) Audit Standards should cover several key components of elections: the Election system itself (internal controls, chain of custody of ballots); Machine/Technology accuracy and signature verification; Registration processes; post-election tabulation of ballots. Independence and transparency are so important to emphasize. Perhaps you could create a training program for independent election auditors... a new profession (?)
