Dr Paquette has also published several peer-reviewed articles about the algorithmic manipulation of voter rolls. Here is the latest: https://www.researchgate.net/publication/404040151_System_Reliability_Analysis_Impact_of_Structural_Anomalies_in_State_Voter_Registration_Systems. This nefarious infiltration of our voter rolls may make the databases unauditable, necessitating that they br scrapped and reconstructed from scratch with nationwide in-person re-registration, at which time each person desiring to register to vote will be required to do so in person with documentary proof of identity and citizenship.
