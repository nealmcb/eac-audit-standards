# EAC-2026-0067-0041 — Robert Kufert

**Date:** 2026-05-20
**Docket:** EAC-2026-0067
**Source:** [Regulations.gov](https://www.regulations.gov/comment/EAC-2026-0067-0041)

---

The Voluntary National Standards for Election Audits – A Practical Guide is an excellent general standards document. It is deficient in that it doesn't address the specific areas where fraudulent activity is most likely to occur. There are 4 that I'm aware of; Vote by Mail (VBM), UOCAVA (ballots sent from outside the country), Catching non-citizens at registration and removing non-citizens who are already registered. Both VBM & UOCAVA currently have insufficient controls. Areas that should be improved with audit standards include envelop/ballot production and distribution, intake and processing center security, ballot storage security, reconciliation and the need for voter ID inclusion. For UOCAVA, there is a lack of domestic address control to confirm county address provided is residential and associated with the voter's family. All 4 ways people can register - with Driver's LIcense (DL), with Social Security number but no DL, No ID and Federal Postcard Application - all need enhanced controls to prevent non-citizens from registering to vote. Voter roll clean-up guidelines are also needed specifying frequency and methods for identifying and removing non-citizens and inactive voters.
