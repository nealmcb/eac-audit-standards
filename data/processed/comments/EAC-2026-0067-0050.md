# EAC-2026-0067-0050 — Jeff Schaeper

**Date:** 2026-05-20
**Docket:** EAC-2026-0067

---

I have read the proposed EAC audit standards. The most recent version is significantly better than the previous version. However it does need to have better clarity on some matters. - There needs to be a different group that audits elections. Right now there is a "fox guarding the henhouse" as far a indepent audit. In many states there is an Inspector General of Auditor General position that is independent of the election bureau. This would satisfy the segregation of duties criteria for accounting principles. - It must be explicit that all election artifacts must be available for audit. This satisfies the accounting principle of materiality. - Some audits must be performed during the certification period so irregularities can be seen and taken into account when certifying an election. Accounting principle of Timeliness - If states do not have a robust audit process in place they should not be eligible for HAVA funding until brought into compliance. I am currently a canvassor for Oakland County Michigan. I have seen where timely complete audits would add more trust to election results. I am a retired Finance Manager with over 35 years in the automotive industry and have been in charge of Internal Controls. Most recently I worked on Michigan Fair Election report on Michigan's HAVA report and a request for a true audit according to GAGAS/GAAP standards. Incorporating the above suggestions would greatly help with conducting meaningful audits vs. pro-forma "check the box" audits.
