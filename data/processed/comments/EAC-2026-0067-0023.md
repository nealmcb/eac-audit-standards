# EAC-2026-0067-0023 — Anonymous Anonymous

**Date:** 2026-05-20
**Docket:** EAC-2026-0067
**Source:** [Regulations.gov](https://www.regulations.gov/comment/EAC-2026-0067-0023)

---

It is essential that election audits be done by an independent agency or third party separate from the election administrator. The vendor paid to report the vote totals should not also be the auditor to verify its reporting was accurate. Likewise, when the Secretary of State conducts the elections, the SOS and staff should not be the ones auditing the elections—nor investigating and adjudicating election complaints. When the henhouse guard fox has chicken feathers stuck in his teeth as he touts that he oversaw the “most secure elections in world history,” do you really want to trust him to be the one to go in and count how many chickens are left inside? No. Independent audits are essential.
