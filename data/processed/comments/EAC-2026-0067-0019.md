# EAC-2026-0067-0019 — Diane Vernesoni

**Date:** 2026-05-20
**Docket:** EAC-2026-0067

---

I believe that the auditing of ballots after an election should be done independently. They must reconcile as to votes cast and ballots distributed. No result that does not reconcile should be accepted as a true result of the election. Correct precinct results are paramount to the election process.
