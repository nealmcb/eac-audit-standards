An excellent draft report! However, the following seem to be missing: (1) Any discussion of statistical audits (e.g. an RLA), including how to explain the results of such an audit to a non-technical audience. (2) A discussion of the fact that a statistical audit may involve a variable amount of work, depending on chance and on how close the election outcome is. (3) Advice on choosing *which* contests to audit, if there aren't enough available resources to audit them all. (4) Advice on choosing ballots at random for a statistical audit. Thanks!
