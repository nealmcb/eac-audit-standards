Overall, I support national election audit standards because I think transparent and credible audits can help people trust election results more. When they are done well, they give the public actual evidence that election outcomes match the ballots that were cast, which is really important at this time. With how divided people are on voting issues right now, having that clarity feels like one of the better ways to strengthen confidence in elections without making voting harder for eligible citizens. However, one thing I would strongly encourage is a bigger emphasis on language that is simple for the public. I think Audit procedures should help confirm accurate election outcomes without becoming so complicated or burdensome that smaller jurisdictions struggle to carry them out effectively. So, I would encourage election offices to release short public summaries that explain the audit method, what was reviewed, and what conclusions the public should take from it. If people do not understand how the audit works or what the results actually show, it becomes harder for the process to build trust. And I feel like that clear communication matters just as much as technical accuracy. So by doing this, it will further build trust and confidence in voters and eliminate that ambiguity. Overall, though, I support these proposed standards and encourage the EAC to finalize them in a way that promotes transparency and public trust while still being practical for different states and local election offices.
