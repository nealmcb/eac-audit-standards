# EAC-2026-0067-0032 — Rosario Zarb-Cousin

**Date:** 2026-05-20
**Docket:** EAC-2026-0067
**Source:** [Regulations.gov](https://www.regulations.gov/comment/EAC-2026-0067-0032)

---

An independent audit by a third party should be performed at least annually. The scope of such audits should include review or testing of general and security controls of the various vendors of the election systems. Audits should include scope, criteria, testing results, recommendations and summary of actions taken.
