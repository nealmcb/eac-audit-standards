**Date:** 2026-05-20
**Docket:** EAC-2026-0067

---

I appreciate the work done on the Election Audit Standards and sincerely hope that these are steps toward election integrity and transparency in the states. I am concerned that including the term voluntary in the name of the regulation will result in many states basically ignoring this regulation. I realize that this is not a mandate, however, using other language such as Best Practices or Standards for Achieving Secure Elections would encourage these regulations to be used within the states to achieve proper and reasonable goals for accurate and secure elections. Recommendations such as a procedure for results to be able to be verified by a manual hand count and audit of ballots by valid voters should be included. Thank you for your consideration.
