# EAC-2026-0067-0004 — Michelle Burchill

**Date:** 2026-05-20
**Docket:** EAC-2026-0067
**Source:** [Regulations.gov](https://www.regulations.gov/comment/EAC-2026-0067-0004)

---

Working in the elections field for several years and cycles has given me more trust in the system. With that said, I feel there should be additional audits in some way. Many jurisdictions don't do comprehensive audits unless something went wrong. It is a good thing to see an outside organization have an expertise and ability to audit processes and give an honest evaluation and recommendation of improvement. I love the option of a separate tabulation audit to ensure all ballots, not just a small sample are being counted corrected.
