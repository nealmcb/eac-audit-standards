# EAC-2026-0067-0054 — Eileen Watts

**Date:** 2026-05-20
**Docket:** EAC-2026-0067

---

I would like to submit the following suggestions for Regulations.gov and the EAC to implement in elections: 1) shift the administration of audits from entities within the election system to an Independent Audit Committee 2) add additional reporting on county performance during the audit, beyond verification of results 3) ensure auditors have access to all related election artifacts, with appropriate protections to maintain voter confidentiality I believe that Honest, Accurate and Transparent Elections will build trust in U.S. Elections for American citizens and this is one step to secure our elections.
