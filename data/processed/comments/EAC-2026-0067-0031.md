# EAC-2026-0067-0031 — Rosario Zarb-Cousin

**Date:** 2026-05-20
**Docket:** EAC-2026-0067
**Source:** [Regulations.gov](https://www.regulations.gov/comment/EAC-2026-0067-0031)

---

All election audits should be done by a third party to ensure audits are non-partisan and transparent. The report should clearly state the scope, testing criteria, results and be made available to the public.
