Comment on Election Audit Standards issued by the U.S. Election Assistance
Commission on February 23, 2026.

-  From William J. Kresse*

I applaud the U.S. Election Assistance Commission (“Commission”) in issuing the
proposed “Election Audit Standards.” These Standards are thorough and show that much
thought was put into creating these.

I am particularly pleased to see that all of these matters are addressed as “Topics”:

  Objective
  Professional
  E(cid:431)ective
  Secure
  Accountable

And that within these Topics, the matters of Ethics, Impartiality, Independence,
Competence, and Transparency are discussed. These matters are particularly vital if
election audits are to promote conﬁdence in our elections.

If I may, I would like to suggest that in the near future the Commission consider the
credentialing of those conducting or at least overseeing election audits. While such
qualities as Transparency are important in gaining conﬁdence, public conﬁdence relies not
just on “how” an election audit is conducted, but also on “who” is conducting it.

Credentialing election auditors would ensure the public that those conducting or
overseeing election audits are competent in performing the task. The public would be
assured that based on a combination of education, training, background, and testing,
election audits are in the hands of people that the Commission has deemed to be
competent for the undertaking.

Additionally, credentialing election auditors will create a mechanism for holding election
auditors accountable for any violations of professional standards. As with any other
credentialed profession, the credentialing body would be able to discipline those found to
have willfully violated the professional standards.

Going forward, I would suggest that public conﬁdence in our elections can be enhanced
through professional election audits. However, for the public to perceive these election
audits to be professional, they must be conducted by a cadre of professional election
auditors. In order to demonstrate to the public that election audits are being conducted by

professionals, the Commission should begin the task of setting in place the standards and
the mechanisms to credential professional election auditors.
