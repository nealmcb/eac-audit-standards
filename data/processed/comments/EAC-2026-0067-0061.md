# EAC-2026-0067-0061 — Kurt Hyde

**Date:** 2026-05-20
**Docket:** EAC-2026-0067
**Source:** [Regulations.gov](https://www.regulations.gov/comment/EAC-2026-0067-0061)

---

I believe election audits would be much more effective if, rather than random choices of precincts to audit, the precincts to audit were chosen by the candidates. Each candidate who gets at least 10% of the vote in a district with 75,000 or more registered voters should be allowed to select one precinct in that district for an audit. The audit should include a recount of the paper ballots for that one contest and optionally include an audit of the voters who are on record as having voted in that precinct to ensure they exist and they actually voted. Additionally, such audits should be open to the public without requiring training or appointments. Video and audio recordings should be allowed.
