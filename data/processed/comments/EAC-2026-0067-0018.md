# EAC-2026-0067-0018 — Christine Welborn

**Date:** 2026-05-20
**Docket:** EAC-2026-0067

---

It is critical that elections are audited by an independent auditor. We cannot allow an agency such as the Secretary of State's office to audit itself. The auditor must have sufficient knowledge of election systems, but must be an outsider to ensure accuracy and transparency. Thank you.
